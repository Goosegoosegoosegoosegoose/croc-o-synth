Croc-O-Synth

A click-through prototype of the kid firmware, laid out like the real FM-1 panel. Each button opens a lesson.

Tap the screen to wake the croc. Drag a knob, or tap its right or left side. Hold HOME to say bye bye. Hold GLO to name the croc.

For the grown-up

EDIT
Teeth: wave shape (sine, saw, square). Rows of teeth are unison voices, x1 to x3 (top, bottom, back row). Crooked is the detune between them
ENV
Snap or yawn: attack, decay, sustain, release on knobs 1 to 4
LFO
Tail wag: speed, depth, and what it tickles. Tail is pitch (vibrato), belly is volume (tremolo), snoot is the filter (wah)
FX
Swamp echo and cave: delay amount and time, reverb
SEQ, PLAY
Lily pad steps: an eight-step sequencer
OCT
Small croc, croc, big croc: three octaves, all inside the FM-1 speaker's range
PRESETS
Surprise: five sounds plus her own, kept as My Croc
ALGORITHM
Jaws: filter cutoff, on every page
SELECT
Hop speed: tempo
MASTER
Volume, with a ceiling

SCL, ARP, REC and SAVE are not built yet and show a placeholder. The sound is a browser stand-in for the FM-1's engine. On a computer, the keys A to ; also play notes. The croc says goodbye after three quiet minutes.
